# Copy Analysis & Suggestions: https://www.bagira.bg/
**Date:** 2026-03-18
**Page Type:** E-commerce Homepage + Product Pages
**Copy Score: 24/100**

---

## Executive Summary

Bagira's website copy is almost entirely absent. The homepage has no headline, no value proposition, no persuasive text — it functions as a product database rather than a marketing channel. Product pages rely on auto-generated meta descriptions with emoji and basic spec sheets. Category pages have zero descriptive content.

The strongest copy on the entire digital presence is actually on Facebook, where posts use conversational Bulgarian with rhetorical questions and emotional appeals. This disconnect between the website (robotic) and social media (warm) represents a major brand consistency issue but also reveals the brand's natural voice.

The copy that does exist suffers from three fundamental problems: (1) it tells nothing about WHY to shop at Bagira, (2) it uses generic e-commerce language that any store could claim, and (3) it lacks any emotional or benefit-driven messaging. Fixing the copy alone — without any design changes — could increase conversion by 15-25%.
