# Brand Voice Guidelines
## БАГИРА (Bagira) — Technical Hypermarket
### Analysis Date: 2026-03-18

---

## Voice Summary

Bagira currently communicates with a functional, no-frills voice that prioritizes product information over personality. The brand oscillates between a dry, catalog-like tone on its website and a warmer, lifestyle-oriented tone on Facebook. There is no unified brand voice — the website reads like a database, while social media attempts to be a friendly home improvement advisor. This disconnect represents both a weakness (inconsistency) and an opportunity (the Facebook voice is closer to what the brand should be).

---

## Voice Dimensions

### Formal <-----> Casual: 5/10

The brand sits in the middle — neither stiff nor relaxed. Website copy is functional and transactional, while Facebook posts lean warmer.

**Evidence:**
- Website tagline: "Пазарувате бързо, лесно и изгодно от вашия дом!" — Informal but not personal, uses second-person plural (formal "you" in Bulgarian)
- Facebook post: "Кой казва, че за голяма промяна в дома е нужен голям бюджет?!" — Conversational, rhetorical question, engaging
- Instagram bio: "Online site bagira.bg,строителство,обков,домашни потреби" — Keyword-stuffed, no personality whatsoever
- Product meta descriptions use template: "Вземете сега ✔️...✔️ - на топ цена от 🔨" — Emoji usage suggests casual intent but template format feels robotic
- TikTok bio: Uses emojis freely — most casual channel

### Serious <-----> Playful: 4/10

Mostly serious with occasional attempts at warmth on social media. No humor, no wordplay, no personality-driven content.

**Evidence:**
- Homepage: Pure product catalog with zero personality or storytelling
- Facebook: "Малки промени с голям ефект" (Small changes with big effect) — Attempt at inspirational tone
- Product descriptions: Purely functional, spec-driven
- No 404 page personality (just a standard error)
- TikTok: "POV: Колегата е пристрасен към коледна украса" — Most playful content, employee humor
- No brand humor, puns, or creative language anywhere on the website

### Technical <-----> Simple: 6/10

Moderately simple. Product descriptions include technical specs but the overall language is accessible. However, there's no effort to educate or simplify — specs are simply listed without context.

**Evidence:**
- Product pages list specs (e.g., "7 к.с. 208CC") without explaining what they mean for the buyer
- Category names are straightforward: "Машини", "Инструменти", "ВиК и Баня"
- No jargon-heavy content, but also no educational content that would help beginners
- The "ОТ МАЙСТОРА" (From the Craftsman) nav section exists but has no content — suggesting aspiration toward expertise
- Meta descriptions use simple language: "Изберете от над 1304 продукта на цени, започващи от 0.33 €"

### Reserved <-----> Bold: 3/10

Quite reserved. The brand makes few claims, takes no strong positions, and has a generic presence that could belong to any hardware store.

**Evidence:**
- Tagline makes no bold claims: "Shop quickly, easily and affordably" — three adjectives every store could claim
- No "why us" messaging, no differentiation statements
- No competitive positioning ("better than X")
- No bold guarantees or promises on the website
- Facebook content is pleasant but avoids strong opinions
- No brand manifesto or mission statement visible anywhere
- The brand name "Bagira" (from Jungle Book panther) is bold, but this personality is completely unused in communications

## Brand Personality

### Primary Archetype: The Guide (weak embodiment)
Bagira positions itself as a place to find tools and solutions, which aligns with The Guide archetype. However, the execution is incomplete — a true Guide provides knowledge, education, and step-by-step help. Bagira currently provides products but not guidance.

**Evidence for Guide:**
- "ОТ МАЙСТОРА" (From the Craftsman) section — aspires to guide customers
- Broad product range organized by need (home, garden, construction)
- Physical store with consultants available
- Facebook content attempts educational tone ("How to improve your home")

**Archetype fit: Weak** — Has Guide aspirations but lacks the content (guides, tutorials, expert advice) to fully embody it.

### Secondary Archetype: The Friend (emerging on social media)
The Facebook voice shows Friend characteristics — warm, approachable, community-building.

**Evidence:**
- "Споделете с нас своя опит" (Share your experience with us)
- "Кой казва, че за голяма промяна в дома е нужен голям бюджет?!" — Friendly, inclusive language
- TikTok employee content — humanizes the brand

**Recommendation:** Fully commit to **The Friend-Guide** hybrid: A knowledgeable, approachable local expert who helps you succeed with your projects. Think "helpful neighbor who happens to know everything about tools."

## Vocabulary

### Words Currently Used
**Action words:** Поръчайте (Order), Вземете (Get/Take), Изберете (Choose), Купете (Buy)
**Descriptive words:** Бързо (Fast), Лесно (Easy), Изгодно (Affordable), Топ цена (Top price)
**Value words:** None consistently used
**Category terms:** Машини, Инструменти, Строителство, ВиК, Градина

### Words They Should Start Using
**Expertise words:** Препоръчваме (We recommend), Съветваме (We advise), Нашите експерти (Our experts), Най-подходящ за (Best suited for)
**Trust words:** Гаранция (Guarantee), Проверено (Verified), Надеждно (Reliable), Доверие (Trust)
**Local pride words:** Стара Загора, Вашият магазин (Your store), На място (On-site), Лично (Personal)
**Emotion words:** Гордост (Pride), Удовлетворение (Satisfaction), Увереност (Confidence), Лесно (Easy)

### Words to Avoid
- Generic e-commerce filler: "Топ цена" (every store claims this)
- Keyword-stuffed lists (current Instagram bio style)
- Impersonal third-person references
- Overly formal Bulgarian (книжовен стил)
- "Електронен магазин" (online store) — says nothing distinctive

### Signature Phrases (Recommended)
- **"Вашият местен експерт"** — Your local expert (positioning tagline)
- **"От майстор за майстори"** — From a craftsman for craftsmen
- **"На място и онлайн"** — In-store and online
- **"БАГИРА знае"** — Bagira knows (for expert advice content)
- **"Проверено от БАГИРА"** — Verified by Bagira (for product recommendations)

## Voice Chart

| Our Voice IS | Our Voice IS NOT |
|---|---|
| **Knowledgeable** — We know our products and guide you to the right choice | **Lecturing** — We don't talk down or over-explain the obvious |
| **Friendly** — We talk like a helpful neighbor, not a corporation | **Unprofessional** — Casual doesn't mean sloppy or careless |

| **Practical** — We focus on solutions, not fluff | **Cold** — Practical doesn't mean robotic; we care about your project's success |
| **Honest** — We recommend what's best for you, even if it's not the most expensive | **Salesy** — We don't pressure; we inform and let you decide |
| **Confident** — We stand behind our products and expertise | **Arrogant** — Confidence comes from experience, not from putting others down |

## Writing Guidelines

### Do's
- **Lead with the benefit, then give the specs.** "Подрязвайте градината за минути — с косачка 2000W, 40см работна ширина" instead of just "Косачка 2000W, 40см"
- **Use "ти/вие" (you) more than "ние" (we).** Make the customer the hero, not the brand
- **Include a recommendation or expert tip** whenever possible. "Нашият съвет: За дворове над 200м², изберете модел с кош за трева"
- **Write in Bulgarian that sounds natural spoken aloud.** Read your copy out loud — if it sounds stiff, rewrite it
- **Use contractions and natural Bulgarian speech patterns** — avoid overly formal book language
- **Reference Stara Zagora and local context** where relevant — weather, seasonal needs, local events
- **End social media posts with a question or invitation** to encourage comments
- **Use emoji sparingly on social media** (1-3 per post maximum), avoid on website

### Don'ts
- **Don't write keyword-stuffed bios or descriptions** — "строителство,обков,домашни потреби,уреди за дома,машини" is not a sentence
- **Don't use template copy without customization** — Each product description should feel human-written
- **Don't ignore the "why"** — Don't just say "Нов продукт!" without explaining why the customer should care
- **Don't post content without a clear purpose** — Every post should educate, engage, or sell (not just fill a schedule)
- **Don't use TikTok watermarks on Instagram** — Create native content for each platform
- **Don't use excessive emoji in product descriptions** — "🔨✔️➤" looks spammy, not professional
- **Don't avoid bold claims when backed by truth** — If you have the best selection in Stara Zagora, say so
- **Don't copy competitor language** — "Топ цена" is what everyone says; find your own way to communicate value

## Brand Messaging Hierarchy

### Tagline (Recommended)
**Current:** "Пазарувате бързо, лесно и изгодно от вашия дом!" (Shop quickly, easily and affordably from your home!)
**Recommended:** "БАГИРА — Всичко за дома, градината и майстора. На място и онлайн."
(Bagira — Everything for home, garden, and the craftsman. In-store and online.)

**Alternative options:**
- "Вашият технически хипермаркет в Стара Загора" (Your technical hypermarket in Stara Zagora)
- "БАГИРА знае от какво имате нужда" (Bagira knows what you need)

### Value Propositions
1. **Широк избор:** Над 15,000 продукта в 12 категории — от инструменти до осветление, от градинска техника до бои (Broad selection: 15,000+ products across 12 categories)
2. **Местен експерт:** Посетете ни в Стара Загора за лични съвети от нашите специалисти (Local expert: Visit us in Stara Zagora for personal advice)
3. **Удобство:** Поръчайте онлайн с доставка до вратата или вземете от магазина (Convenience: Order online with delivery or pick up in-store)
4. **Достъпност:** Конкурентни цени + финансиране на изплащане от ДСК (Affordability: Competitive prices + installment financing from DSK)

### Elevator Pitch
"БАГИРА е техническият хипермаркет на Стара Загора — от над 20 години помагаме на домашни майстори, професионалисти и градинари да намерят точно това, от което имат нужда. С над 15,000 продукта на място и онлайн, лични консултации от нашите експерти и бърза доставка, ние сме повече от магазин — ние сме вашият партньор във всеки проект."

### Boilerplate
"БАГИРА ООД е технически хипермаркет със седалище в Стара Загора, бул. Патриарх Евтимий 39. Компанията предлага над 15,000 продукта в категории като машини, инструменти, строителни материали, ВиК, осветление, градинска техника и домашни потреби. С физически магазин и онлайн платформа (bagira.bg), БАГИРА обслужва както крайни клиенти, така и професионалисти. Компанията предлага консултации на място, бърза доставка и финансиране на изплащане. За повече информация: bagira.bg | 0886 886 332."

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions
1. **Rewrite the Instagram bio** using the recommended brand voice (see copy sample above)
2. **Add a welcome headline to the homepage** with brand personality (not just the logo)
3. **Update the tagline** from generic to distinctive
4. **Create 3 brand voice templates** for: product descriptions, social media posts, and email subject lines

### Voice Evolution Roadmap
1. **Month 1:** Align website copy to the Friend-Guide voice (homepage, about page, category intros)
2. **Month 2:** Launch the "ОТ МАЙСТОРА" blog with expert content in the brand voice
3. **Month 3:** Train social media on voice guidelines; create content templates
4. **Month 4-6:** Extend voice to email marketing, in-store signage, and customer service scripts

### Consistency Improvements
1. **Create a 1-page voice cheat sheet** for anyone writing Bagira content (the Voice Chart + Do's/Don'ts above)
2. **Assign a single voice owner** who reviews all content before publishing
3. **Audit and rewrite the top 20 product descriptions** as models for the rest
4. **Create platform-specific tone guidelines:** Website (professional-warm), Facebook (warm-engaging), Instagram (visual-aspirational), TikTok (playful-authentic)
