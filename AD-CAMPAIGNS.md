# Ad Campaigns: БАГИРА (Bagira) Technical Hypermarket
**URL:** https://www.bagira.bg/
**Date:** 2026-03-18
**Business Type:** E-commerce — Hardware / Tools / Home Improvement
**Primary Objective:** E-commerce Sales + Local Store Traffic
**Recommended Platforms:** Google Ads (50%), Meta Ads (35%), TikTok (15%)

---

## Campaign Strategy Overview

Bagira's advertising strategy should leverage its two strongest assets: (1) a physical store in Stara Zagora with no direct national chain competition, and (2) a 32K Facebook community that can be retargeted. The current ad infrastructure includes Google Ads (AW-17790919805), Facebook Pixel (3904189599802154), and TheMarketer — suggesting some paid activity, but the strategy needs structure.

We recommend a three-tier approach: (1) Google Search/Shopping campaigns capturing high-intent buyers searching for specific products and local stores, (2) Meta campaigns for retargeting website visitors and growing awareness through the existing 32K follower base, and (3) TikTok Spark Ads boosting employee-created organic content to build brand awareness with younger audiences.

Recommended starting budget: **1,000-1,500€/month** across all platforms, with 60% allocated to Google (highest intent) and 40% to Meta/TikTok (awareness + retargeting).

## Creative Brief for Designers

### Brand Guidelines for Ads
- **Primary color:** Red (#FF0000) — Bagira brand red
- **Secondary:** White background, black text
- **Logo:** Always include BAGIRA red logo in top-left or top-center
- **Font:** Bold, clear, readable at mobile sizes
- **Photography style:** Product on clean background OR lifestyle (product in use)
- **Price display:** Always show EUR + BGN, crossed-out original price for promotions
- **Mandatory elements:** BAGIRA logo, price, product image, CTA

### Image Specifications Needed
| Format | Dimensions | Usage |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Facebook/Instagram Feed | 1080×1080px | Primary ad format |
| Instagram Stories | 1080×1920px | Story placements |
| Google Display | 1200×628px | Display network |
| Google Shopping | 1000×1000px (white bg) | Shopping feed |
| TikTok | 1080×1920px (vertical video) | In-feed ads |

### Video Specifications
- **Length:** 15-30 seconds (TikTok: 15-25s, Meta: 15-30s)
- **Format:** MP4, H.264
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (Stories/TikTok), 1:1 (Feed)
- **Text overlays:** Product name + price, always visible
- **Captions:** Required (most viewers watch without sound)
- **First 3 seconds:** Must include hook + brand logo
