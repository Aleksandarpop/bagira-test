## 3. Strategy & Approach

### Strategic Framework: "Местният Експерт Онлайн" (The Local Expert, Online)

Our strategy positions Bagira as the trusted local expert who also happens to have a comprehensive online store. This differentiates from national chains (impersonal) and online-only stores (no physical presence).

### Phase 1: Foundation & Quick Wins (Month 1-2)

**Website Optimization:**
- Implement transparent shipping costs across all product pages
- Add trust bar to header (delivery, financing, returns)
- Create text-based H1 and hero section for homepage
- Fix Cyrillic URL issues with 301 redirects
- Add customer review system (replace fake 5-star ratings)
- Add related products and cross-sell functionality
- Optimize all images (WebP conversion, lazy loading, srcset)

**SEO Quick Wins:**
- Write category descriptions for all 12 major categories (300-500 words each)
- Fix empty homepage H1 and meta descriptions
- Add LocalBusiness schema markup
- Create local landing page: "Технически магазин Стара Загора"
- Submit optimized sitemap with lastmod dates

**Social Media Foundation:**
- Redesign Instagram profile (new bio, Story Highlights)
- Create branded content templates (Canva)
- Set up Meta Business Suite for cross-platform management
- Respond to all existing Facebook reviews
- Launch "reintroduction" campaign on Instagram

**Expected Quick Win Results:**
- 20-30% reduction in homepage bounce rate
- 30-50% increase in checkout completion (shipping transparency)
- 10-15% increase in add-to-cart rate

### Phase 2: Growth & Content (Month 3-4)

**Content Marketing Launch:**
- Publish 4 buying guides ("Как да изберем бормашина", "Как да изберем косачка", etc.)
- Launch "ОТ МАЙСТОРА" blog section with 2 posts/week
- Create 4 video product demonstrations (filmed in-store)
- Build seasonal content: "Пролетна градина" collection page

**Social Media Growth:**
- Execute 30-day content calendar (Facebook 5x/week, Instagram 3x/week, TikTok 2x/week)
- Launch #БагираМайстори user-generated content campaign
- Run first Instagram growth campaign (cross-promoted from Facebook)
- Begin employee-driven TikTok content series

**Email Marketing Setup:**
- Implement cart abandonment email sequence
- Create welcome email series for new subscribers
- Launch newsletter with seasonal content

**Expected Growth Results:**
- 50-100% increase in organic search traffic
- Instagram growth to 500+ followers
- 10-15% increase in repeat purchases via email

### Phase 3: Scale & Optimize (Month 5-6)

**Paid Advertising:**
- Launch Google Ads campaigns targeting local + national keywords
- Create Facebook/Instagram retargeting campaigns
- Implement dynamic product ads from catalog
- Test Google Shopping campaigns

**Advanced SEO:**
- Build link profile through local business directories
- Create competitor comparison pages
- Expand content to cover all major product categories
- Implement FAQ schema across product pages

**Conversion Optimization:**
- A/B test homepage hero variations
- Optimize product page CTAs based on data
- Implement exit-intent offers
- Launch loyalty/rewards program

**Expected Scale Results:**
- 100-200% increase in online revenue vs. baseline
- 3-4% overall conversion rate (up from 1-1.5%)
- 1,000+ Instagram followers
- Top 3 Google ranking for "технически магазин Стара Загора"
